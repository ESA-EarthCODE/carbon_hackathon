# EO-LINCS

[EO-LINCS](https://www.eo-lincs.org/) stands for **Earth Observation, Local and Integrated data eNquiry for Terrestrial Carbon Science**. Funded under ESA's Carbon Science Cluster, the project brings together satellite observations, local measurements, and modelling to support research into the terrestrial carbon cycle.

Its focus includes understanding how differences in spatial scale, timing, and representativeness affect the use of these complementary sources. The hackathon will use EO-LINCS tools and workflows to explore carbon-science questions.

## Data access with xcube-multistore

EO-LINCS develops reusable data pipelines. Its [xcube-multistore package](https://www.eo-lincs.org/xcube-multistore.html) coordinates multiple `xcube` data-store plugins to access and combine data into analysis-ready cubes.

Examples of supported sources include:

- Copernicus Land Monitoring Service products for land cover and vegetation.
- ESA Climate Change Initiative products, including biomass, soil moisture, and fire.
- Sentinel observations through STAC services and GEDI canopy measurements.
- ICOS Data Portal flux products and Copernicus climate data.
