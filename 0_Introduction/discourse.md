# EarthCODE Discourse

[EarthCODE Discourse](https://discourse-earthcode.eox.at/) is the community forum for EarthCODE users, science clusters, and working groups. Use it to ask questions, share ideas, and keep hackathon discussions available after the live sessions.

## Carbon Hackathon discussions

Post questions about datasets, EO-LINCS workflows, the DeepESDL platform, notebooks, technical issues, or ideas you would like to explore during the hackathon here:

https://discourse-earthcode.eox.at/t/esa-carbon-data-cubes-hackathon/165 

You can also use the [Science Clusters & Working Groups category](https://discourse-earthcode.eox.at/c/science-clusters-working-groups/6) for science discussions.
